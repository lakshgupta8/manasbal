Adds the `Button` (primary, secondary and ghost; md and sm; optional icon), `TextField` (label, "(required)", hint, error, and a password version with a show button) and `Checkbox` (never ticked unless the caller says so; error row) components, themed by tokens only. New meaning-layer colours for focus rings, hover, disabled controls and errors (`color.feedback.error`, `color.feedback.error-border`), with their contrast pairs checked, and a `data-theme="dark"` block in `theme.css` so part of a page can use the dark theme.
