The home page welcomes visitors, shows the categories as tiles and the first products as cards. Each category has its own page (`/[locale]/c/[slug]`) with breadcrumbs, its subcategories, its products 12 to a page and previous and next page links. Loading, error, empty and not-found states say in words what is happening, with an icon; a stopped API shows "This page couldn't load" with "Try again".
